## English

### Fixes

- **Auto Sprint**: Fixed the auto sprint overlay button not triggering sprint in-game. The injected key event was re-entering the overlay keybind handler in a loop, preventing it from reaching the game.
- **Overlay buttons**: Fixed overlay buttons being draggable outside HUD editor mode.

---

## 简体中文

### 修复

- **自动疾跑**：修复自动疾跑悬浮按钮点击后无法触发疾跑的问题。注入的按键事件被悬浮窗按键拦截器循环拦截，导致按键无法到达游戏。
- **悬浮按钮**：修复非 HUD 编辑模式下仍可拖动悬浮按钮的问题。
